## What This Website Is Used For

BetterSanPascualMasbate serves as an informational hub to bridge the gap in public data accessibility on Burias Island. It focuses on three primary pillars:

1.  **Civic Accessibility**: Centralizing municipal support programs, agricultural aid, and medical resources.
2.  **Public Accountability**: Providing a searchable index of local ordinances, resolutions, and municipal budgets.
3.  **Local Preservation**: Promoting San Pascual's unique heritage and ecological landmarks.

## What the Portal Contains

### 1. Municipal Infrastructure & Projects
- **Public Infrastructure Registry**: A detailed search index of municipal buildings, roads, and upcoming local projects.
- **Project Transparency**: Tracking local fund allocations and administrative mandates for better public accountability.

### 2. Population & Statistics
- **Demographic Insights**: Real-time population trend analysis using official PSA census data and LGU growth projections.
- **Barangay Breakdown**: Detailed statistics for all 22 barangays, including population counts and localized demographics.

### 3. Open Transparency Registry
- **Ordinances & Resolutions**: Verifiable public domain records detailing island carrying capacities, coastal clean-ups, and agricultural response funds.
- **Municipal Budgets**: Scannable logs showing local fund allocations to healthcare, infrastructure, and copra farming.

### 4. Island Landmark Guides & Chronicles
- **Eco-Tourism Virtual Tour**: Guides to pristine geological destinations including Sombrero Island, Tinalisayan sandbar, and Animasola.
- **Historical Timeline**: A scannable chronological layout tracking local milestones from the 1586 parish founding to today.

### 5. Municipal Services Handbook
- **Health & Wellness**: Guides to basic diagnostics, free senior maintenance medicines, and childhood immunizations.
- **Social Services**: Information on senior pensions, PWD benefits, and educational scholarship portals.
- **Emergency Responder Hotlines**: Quick-dial contact details for MDRRMO, PNP, BFP, and local medical facilities.

## Technical Architecture

- **Framework**: React 18 with TypeScript for type-safe development.
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS for a modern, responsive "editorial" design.
- **Visualization**: Chart.js & react-chartjs-2 for demographic insights.
- **Icons**: Lucide React for consistent, accessible iconography.
- **Optimization**: Granular code splitting and custom skeleton loaders per component.
